White to move.

Board & pieces

  • Board: 8×8 grid, labeled a–h and 1–8.
  • White starts at the bottom: coordinates run from a1 to h8.
  • Pieces: pawn, rook, knight, bishop, queen, king.

How each piece moves

  • Pawn: Moves forward one square, two from the starting rank, and captures diagonally.
  • Rook: Slides any number of squares horizontally or vertically.
  • Knight: Jumps in an L-shape: two squares one way and one square sideways.
  • Bishop: Slides diagonally any number of squares.
  • Queen: Combines rook and bishop movement.
  • King: Moves one square in any direction; castling is allowed if the path is clear and the king is not in check.

Special rules

  • Captures remove the opponent's piece from the board.
  • Check means the king is under attack and must respond.
  • Checkmate ends the game when no legal move can stop the attack.
  • En passant, castling, and pawn promotion are supported in the game logic.
